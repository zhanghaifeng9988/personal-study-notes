

启动脚本存放位置：


开机启动脚本，
nohup /opt/prometheus/prometheus --config.file=/opt/prometheus/prometheus.yml >/opt/prometheus/stdout.log 2> /opt/prometheus/stderr.log &

#node_exporter启动
nohup /opt/node_exporter/node_exporter > /opt/node_exporter/stdout.log 2>/opt/node_exporter/stderr.log &

#mysql_exporte启动
nohup /opt/mysqld_exporter/mysqld_exporter --config.my-cnf=/opt/mysqld_exporter/mysqld_exporter.cnf > /opt/mysqld_exporter/stdout.log 2>/opt/mysqld_exporter/stderr.log &

#启动grafana
systemctl start grafana-server.service  


9090端口：Prometheus的默认端口
9100端口：node_exporter的默认端口
9104端口：mysql_exporter的默认端口
